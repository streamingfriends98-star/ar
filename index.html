Three steps to the reveal

01 · Open on your phone

Scan your generated QR code using the phone’s regular Camera app, then open its link.

02 · Allow the camera

Tap Launch camera experience. Open in Safari or Chrome rather than an in-app browser.

03 · Frame the marker

Point at the complete marker on paper or a second screen. Keep its white margin visible.

The QR opens the website. This marker anchors the 3D visual. They serve different purposes.
